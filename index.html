Sessions

The session timer for therapists.

Add your schedule and stay fully present. Sessions keeps time for you, with discreet wrist taps and glanceable color, so you always know where you are in the hour without looking at a clock.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone and Apple Watch

Starts on its own

Your sessions begin at their scheduled time automatically. No fumbling with timers between clients, and nothing to forget.

Taps, not clocks

One tap on your wrist at the halfway point, two taps when 5 minutes remain, three when time is up. Only you know.

Color you can read across the room

Prop your iPhone on your desk. The full screen drifts from green to red as the hour winds down. A quick glance is all you need.

Syncs with your calendar

Appointments import automatically from your existing calendar. Only start times are read, never names or details.

Insights that grow with you

Weekly patterns, busiest days, and your lifetime hours in session, including the ones from before the app.

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no servers. Everything stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Built for the therapy room

Client names never enter the app. Read the full privacy policy.
