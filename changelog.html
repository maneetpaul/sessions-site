Release notes for Sessions: Therapist Timer

Version 1.0

The first release of Sessions. Everything you need to stay on time and fully present, on iPhone and Apple Watch.

  • Automatic timing. Add your day's sessions, or import them from your calendar, and each one starts on its own at its scheduled time.
  • Discreet wrist taps. Your Apple Watch taps you at the halfway point, at five minutes left, and at the end, so you never look at a clock.
  • Glanceable color. A full-screen desk timer and watch ring shift from green to red as the session winds down.
  • Live on your lock screen. A Live Activity and Dynamic Island keep the countdown in view, and a watch face complication shows your next session.
  • Runs early or late. When a client arrives off schedule, start or restart a session in a tap, keeping its full length or its original end time.
  • Insights. Weekly patterns, busiest days, and your lifetime hours in session, including the ones from before the app.
  • Private and backed up. No accounts and no servers. Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, and restores automatically on a new iPhone.
